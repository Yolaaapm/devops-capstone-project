# User Stories - Customer Account Service

## Story 1: Create a Customer Account
**As a** Marketing Manager
**I need** to be able to create a new customer account
**So that** I can track customer details and purchase history.

## Story 2: Read Account Details
**As a** Customer Service Representative
**I need** to retrieve the details of a specific account
**So that** I can provide support to that customer.

## Story 3: Update Account Information
**As a** Data Entry Clerk
**I need** to update an existing customer's contact information
**So that** our database remains accurate and up-to-date.
